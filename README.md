# 🎮 VoidForge Arcade

A fast-paced HTML5 arcade game hub with two challenging games: **Coin Rush** and **Dungeon Escape**.

## 🕹️ Games

### 🪙 Coin Rush
- Collect falling coins while dodging bombs
- Survive increasingly difficult waves
- Find hearts to restore health
- How high can you score?

### ⚔️ Dungeon Escape
- Navigate through 5 levels of dungeons
- Find the key to unlock each exit
- Avoid enemies patrolling the halls
- Can you escape all 5 levels?

## 🎮 How to Play

### Coin Rush
- Use **← →** arrow keys or **LEFT/RIGHT** buttons to move
- Collect 🪙 coins (+1 point)
- Avoid 💣 bombs (-1 life)
- Grab ❤️ hearts (+1 life)
- Survive 3 lives worth of gameplay!

### Dungeon Escape
- Use **↑ ↓ ← →** arrow keys or **ARROW** buttons to move
- Find the 🔑 key
- Navigate to the 🚪 exit
- Avoid 👾 enemies (👹 on higher levels)
- Complete 5 levels to win!

## 💡 Tips & Tricks

**Coin Rush:**
- Level increases every 8 coins - get faster!
- Missing coins costs a life (bombs don't)
- Plan your movements ahead

**Dungeon Escape:**
- Explore carefully before rushing
- Enemies move faster on higher levels
- Use walls to hide from enemies
